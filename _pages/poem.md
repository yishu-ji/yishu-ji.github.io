I'm reserving this sapce to share one of my favorite poems with those who are still wondering, challenging, pursing, or maybe confusing for the essential meaning of our being.

*The question, O me! so sad, recurring—What good amid these, O me, O life?*

                                       *Answer.*
*That you are here—that life exists and identity,*
*That the powerful play goes on, and you may contribute a verse.*
