I'm reserving this space to share one of my favorite poems with those who are still questioning, challenging, pursing, or maybe confusing at the essential meaning of our beings. [O Me! O Life!](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poems/51568/o-me-o-life) by [Walt_Whitman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walt_Whitman)

*The question, O me! so sad, recurring — What good amid these, O me, O life?*

                                       *Answer.*

*That you are here — that life exists and identity,*
*That the powerful play goes on, and you may contribute a verse.*

Let's contribute a verse. Cheers. 与你共勉
